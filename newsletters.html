Beyond the podium

Engineering under pressure

From simulation to the track

During early testing in Leoben, Austria, a steering algorithm fault sent repeated maximum-steering commands to the car. The team traced and fixed it within the same testing session, reinforcing the importance of real-world validation.

Rebuilding far from home

The car travelled overseas disassembled. Reassembly, brake bleeding, dry checks and a broken 3D printer added to the challenge. The team nevertheless completed scrutineering ahead of schedule in both Switzerland and Portugal.

Inside the team

Many disciplines. One race car.

Mechanical

Structural, Powertrain and Dynamics bring together a carbon-fibre monocoque, twin rear-mounted EMRAX motors, a planetary gearbox and simulation-led suspension, braking and steering development.

Electrical

Controls, Communication and Data Acquisition (CCD), Accumulator Management System (AMS) and Electronic System Integration (ESI) connect telemetry, battery monitoring and fail-safe hardware into one vehicle architecture.

Driverless

Perception interprets camera and LiDAR data; SLAM maps the track and locates the car; Path Planning & Controls turns trajectories into driving commands. SIM-DEV tests the stack in a CARLA-based virtual environment.

Business

Business Plan Presentation, Marketing and Operations connect our engineering to the outside world through business analysis, sponsorships, communications, budgets and team coordination.

2026–27 goals

The road ahead

The next chapter builds on this season's lessons. These are development targets outlined in the newsletter.

Developing the next car

  • Dual in-hub motors to reduce drivetrain weight.
  • A 600V accumulator architecture, up from 400V.
  • Optimised components, targeting a 15 kg chassis weight reduction.
  • Improved accessibility and rain-test compliance.

Raising performance

  • More responsive driverless braking and steering actuators.
  • A target driverless average speed above 60 km/h.
  • Validation of aerodynamics, torsional rigidity and suspension linkage forces.
  • Aiming to win Formula Student AI and Formula Student UK.
